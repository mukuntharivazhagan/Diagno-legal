Option 1: In-App Deletion (Fastest & Immediate)

You can permanently delete your Diagno account and all associated data directly inside the application at any time. In-app deletion takes effect immediately.

1. Open Diagno → 2. Profile → 3. Delete account → 4. Confirm
  1. Launch the Diagno app on your Android device.
  2. Tap your profile avatar or the Profile tab in the navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account Actions section and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the permanent deletion prompt. Your account and cloud records are immediately deleted.

Option 2: Without the App (Email Request)

If you uninstalled the application or cannot access your device, you can request full account deletion via email:

  • Send an email to: diagnoedugame25@gmail.com
  • Sender: Must be sent from the registered email address associated with your Diagno account.
  • Subject line: Delete my Diagno account
  • Timeframe: Email deletion requests are verified and completed within 30 days.

Data Handling Upon Deletion

Data that is deleted

When your deletion request is processed, the following information is permanently erased:

  • Profile Information: Display name, registered email address, and avatar URL.
  • Identity Credentials: User ID (Firebase Authentication UID).
  • Progress & Learning History: Academic level, experience points (XP), clinical rating (CR / ELO), and grand rounds records.
  • Quiz & Game Activity: Daily puzzles completed, accuracy logs, duel match outcomes, streaks, and bookmarks.
  • In-Game Virtual Assets: Unlocked companions/pets, virtual stones, rubies, and gems.
  • Saved Settings: Audio preferences, notification toggles, and user preferences.

Data that is kept

Nothing is kept.

No personal data, user documents (/users/{uid}), or authentication profiles are retained after account deletion.

Backup Retention Period:

Routine encrypted database snapshots and system backups are automatically overwritten and purged within 30 days. After this retention window, no traces of your data remain in backups.

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once deleted, your game progress, clinical duel rating, companion pets, and educational statistics cannot be recovered.
